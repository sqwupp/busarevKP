Choose a trip that fits your mood

01 / NATURE

Wild landscapes

Follow mountain paths, wake up beside alpine lakes and trade busy streets for open skies. Great for travellers who want fresh air and a proper reset.

  • Suggested stay: 5–8 days
  • Best for: hiking and photography
  • Travel pace: active
02 / CULTURE

City & culture

Get lost in old town lanes, visit local markets and build your days around art, architecture and memorable food.

  • Suggested stay: 3–6 days
  • Best for: food and history
  • Travel pace: flexible
03 / SLOW TRAVEL

Coast & calm

Leave room for long breakfasts, swimming spots and sunset walks. A slower itinerary makes space for the small moments.

  • Suggested stay: 6–10 days
  • Best for: couples and friends
  • Travel pace: relaxed
