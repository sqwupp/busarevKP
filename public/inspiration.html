Travel notes

Ideas to take with you

FIELD NOTE 01

Plan less, notice more

Pick one or two anchors for each day, then leave space for unexpected turns. The best memory often starts with an unplanned stop.

FIELD NOTE 02

Travel beyond peak season

Shoulder-season dates can mean a calmer pace and a different view of a destination. Always check local weather and opening dates before booking.

FIELD NOTE 03

Pack for the trip you have

Start with layers, comfortable shoes and the essentials. Build your list around the forecast and activities rather than packing for every possibility.
